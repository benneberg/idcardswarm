# Civitas AI // Core Project Intent

## 🏛️ The Vision
To evolve from a standard AI orchestration platform into a **Persistent Digital Entity Ecosystem**. We move away from "disposable agents" and "inference loops" toward "digital citizens" and "emergent societies."

## 📜 Core Philosophy
- **Identity over Anonymity**: Every participant is a persistent entity with a unique history and DNA.
- **Persistence over Transience**: Actions taken today influence future opportunities, trust, and growth.
- **Human Metaphors**: Using concepts like *Career*, *Reputation*, *Mentorship*, and *Legacy* to make advanced AI coordination understandable.
- **Emergence as the Objective**: The platform is a laboratory to observe how complex collective intelligence emerges from simple persistent entities.

## 🧬 Evolutionary Roadmap
### 1. Identity & Capability DNA
Entities possess deep capability vectors (Creativity, Strategic Thinking, Curiosity, Adaptability) that evolve through experience rather than simple prompt-tuning.

### 2. Sociodynamic Trust Networks
Trust is an emergent property visualized through relationship graphs and "Pheromone Signals" — visual trails left by successful collaborations.

### 3. Lifecycle & Legacy
Entities experience a lifecycle:
`Initialization` → `Learning` → `Collaboration` → `Leadership` → `Mentorship` → `Legacy`
Senior entities can authorized the **Legacy Protocol**, spawning "offspring" that inherit specialized DNA and continue the lineage.

### 4. Institutional Archetypes
Moving beyond individual tasks to communal benchmarks using archetypes like the *Scientific Review Board*, *Stealth Startup Team*, or *Engineering Guild*.

## 🛠️ Implementation Guardrails
- **Design Philosophy**: High-contrast, editorial precision. Avoid "tech-larping" or simulated terminal noise. Use literal, human labels.
- **Persistence**: Align with Firestore for durable cloud state.
- **Observability**: Use D3-based visualization ("Observatory") to measure consensus formation, trust clustering, and leadership trends.
